# Module 1: What is a Supply Chain?

## The Big Idea

A supply chain is the journey of a product from where it is made to where it is bought.

Think about a chocolate bar. Before you eat it, a lot of things happened:

1. Farmers grew cocoa beans
2. Trucks took the beans to a factory
3. The factory made chocolate
4. The chocolate was put in boxes
5. Trucks took the boxes to a store
6. You bought the chocolate bar

That whole journey is the supply chain.

## Why Does It Matter?

If any step goes wrong, you cannot get your chocolate bar.

- If the farm has a bad crop, no beans
- If the truck breaks down, no delivery
- If the store runs out, no chocolate for you

Every business has a supply chain. Managing it well means:
- Customers get what they want
- Companies make money
- Less waste happens

## The 5 Steps of a Supply Chain

Every supply chain has these steps:

### 1. Plan
Decide what to make, how much, and when.

### 2. Source
Find the materials and the people who will supply them.

### 3. Make
Turn the materials into a product.

### 4. Deliver
Move the product to where customers are.

### 5. Return
Handle returns, recycling, or disposal.

## Real Example: A Pizza Shop

Let us trace the supply chain of a pizza:

1. **Plan**: The shop decides how many pizzas to make today
2. **Source**: Buy flour, cheese, tomatoes, pepperoni from suppliers
3. **Make**: Cook the pizza in the oven
4. **Deliver**: Deliver to the customer's house
5. **Return**: Take back the empty box for recycling

## Try It Yourself

Think about your favorite product. Can you trace its supply chain?

Answer these questions:
1. Where was it made?
2. What materials were used?
3. How did it get to the store?
4. How did you get it?

## Key Words

- **Supply chain**: The journey of a product from raw materials to customer
- **Supplier**: A company that provides materials or parts
- **Customer**: The person who buys the product
- **Inventory**: Stuff waiting to be sold or used
