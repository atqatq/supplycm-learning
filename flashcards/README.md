# Flashcards

Flashcards are the engine of remembering. Each deck covers one module's
algorithms: the prompt asks what a method does; the answer is one honest
sentence.

## How to use them

1. **Cover the answer column** (a hand, a card, another window).
2. **Say your answer out loud** before checking - out loud is the point.
3. **Be honest:** got it right = next card; hesitated or wrong = the card
   returns to tomorrow's pile.
4. **Space your reviews:** revisit a deck after 1 day, 3 days, 1 week, 1 month.

## The daily ritual

15 minutes. Ten cards. Every day. This single habit is the difference between
"read about supply chains once" and "know supply chains for life".

## Make your own

Any card you miss twice deserves a handwritten card in your own words - the
weirder and more personal the image on it, the better it sticks.
