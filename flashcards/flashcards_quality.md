# Flashcards: Quality

7 cards. Cover the answer column, say your answer out loud, then check.
Shuffle the deck once you know the order. Revisit after 1 day, 3 days, 1 week, 1 month.

| # | Prompt | Answer |
|---|--------|--------|
| 1 | What does `dpmo` do? | DPMO (Defects Per Million Opportunities): DPMO scales your defect count to 'how many mistakes would we make per million chances?'. |
| 2 | What does `sigma_level` do? | Sigma Level: Sigma level converts DPMO into the famous Six Sigma scale. |
| 3 | What does `process_capability_cp` do? | Process Capability (Cp): Cp asks: IF this process were centered, would its natural spread fit inside the spec limits? It compares the spec width to six standard deviations of the process. |
| 4 | What does `process_capability_cpk` do? | Process Capability (Cpk): Cpk is Cp's honest sibling: it also checks where the process is actually centered. |
| 5 | What does `x_bar_chart` do? | X-Bar Control Chart: The X-bar chart tracks the AVERAGE of small samples over time and draws control limits around it. |
| 6 | What does `r_chart` do? | R Control Chart: The R chart tracks the RANGE (biggest minus smallest) within each sample - it watches the process's consistency, while the X-bar chart watches its average. |
| 7 | What does `p_chart` do? | P Control Chart: The p chart tracks the PROPORTION of defective items in samples - perfect for pass/fail data like '3 of 100 deliveries late'. |
