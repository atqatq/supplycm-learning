# Flashcards: Contracts

3 cards. Cover the answer column, say your answer out loud, then check.
Shuffle the deck once you know the order. Revisit after 1 day, 3 days, 1 week, 1 month.

| # | Prompt | Answer |
|---|--------|--------|
| 1 | What does `revenue_sharing_contract` do? | Revenue Sharing Contract: Instead of paying a high wholesale price, the buyer pays a lower price PLUS a share of the revenue it earns. |
| 2 | What does `buyback_contract` do? | Buyback Contract: The supplier promises to buy back unsold stock at a set price at season's end. |
| 3 | What does `quantity_flexibility_contract` do? | Quantity Flexibility Contract: The buyer may adjust its order up or down by an agreed fraction after seeing early demand - say, plus or minus 20%. |
