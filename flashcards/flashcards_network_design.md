# Flashcards: Network Design

5 cards. Cover the answer column, say your answer out loud, then check.
Shuffle the deck once you know the order. Revisit after 1 day, 3 days, 1 week, 1 month.

| # | Prompt | Answer |
|---|--------|--------|
| 1 | What does `break_even_analysis` do? | Break-Even Analysis: How many units must we sell before the business stops losing money? Fixed costs divided by the margin per unit. |
| 2 | What does `center_of_gravity` do? | Center of Gravity Location: Where should the warehouse go? The center of gravity weights every customer's location by demand volume and returns the balance point - the spot that minimizes total straight-line shipping effort. |
| 3 | What does `facility_location_fixed_cost` do? | Facility Location (Fixed Cost): The real siting problem: each candidate warehouse has a fixed opening cost, shipping costs vary by lane, and you can't open everything. |
| 4 | What does `network_reliability` do? | Network Reliability: Each node works with some probability - what's the chance the NETWORK still functions? This combines component reliabilities and backup edges into one survival probability. |
| 5 | What does `single_source_allocation` do? | Single-Source Allocation: Each customer must be served by exactly ONE facility - no splitting shipments. |
