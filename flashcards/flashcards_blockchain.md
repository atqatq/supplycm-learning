# Flashcards: Traceability & Blockchain

4 cards. Cover the answer column, say your answer out loud, then check.
Shuffle the deck once you know the order. Revisit after 1 day, 3 days, 1 week, 1 month.

| # | Prompt | Answer |
|---|--------|--------|
| 1 | What does `hash_chain` do? | Hash Chain: A chain of digital fingerprints: each event's fingerprint includes the PREVIOUS event's fingerprint, so every record is welded to the one before. |
| 2 | What does `verify_chain` do? | Chain Verification: The audit half of the hash chain: recompute every fingerprint from the records and compare with the stored chain. |
| 3 | What does `provenance_tracking` do? | Provenance Tracking: One product's complete life story, extracted from the stream of all supply chain events: harvested here, shipped there, stored there, sold there. |
| 4 | What does `smart_contract_check` do? | Smart Contract Check: Rules that enforce themselves: temperature below 8, delivery before Friday, quantity within tolerance - this validates any transaction against the rule list and returns pass or explicit violations. |
