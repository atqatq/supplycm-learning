# Flashcards: IoT & Sensors

4 cards. Cover the answer column, say your answer out loud, then check.
Shuffle the deck once you know the order. Revisit after 1 day, 3 days, 1 week, 1 month.

| # | Prompt | Answer |
|---|--------|--------|
| 1 | What does `sensor_data_smoothing` do? | Sensor Data Smoothing: Raw sensor readings jitter - a temperature of 21.3, 21.4, 25.0, 21.2 is probably not a heat wave. |
| 2 | What does `anomaly_detection` do? | IoT Anomaly Detection: This watches a live data stream and flags readings that stray far from the recent window's norm - measured in standard deviations. |
| 3 | What does `realtime_inventory_monitor` do? | Real-Time Inventory Monitor: Connected shelves and bins report levels continuously - this turns those readings into action lists: which SKUs are below their reorder point, and how urgently given their safety stock. |
| 4 | What does `iot_data_aggregation` do? | IoT Data Aggregation: Sensors talk constantly - hundreds of readings per hour, most redundant. |
