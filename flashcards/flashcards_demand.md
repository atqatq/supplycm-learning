# Flashcards: Demand Planning

9 cards. Cover the answer column, say your answer out loud, then check.
Shuffle the deck once you know the order. Revisit after 1 day, 3 days, 1 week, 1 month.

| # | Prompt | Answer |
|---|--------|--------|
| 1 | What does `demand_aggregation` do? | Demand Aggregation: Aggregation rolls daily demand up into bigger buckets - weekly, monthly, or quarterly. |
| 2 | What does `demand_disaggregation` do? | Demand Disaggregation: Disaggregation is the reverse of aggregation: take one big forecast (say, '12,000 units next quarter for the family') and split it across products, regions, or customers using known proportions. |
| 3 | What does `seasonality_index` do? | Seasonality Index: A seasonality index tells you how each period compares to the average: 1.0 is ordinary, 1.3 means 30% above typical, 0.7 means 30% below. |
| 4 | What does `promotional_demand_lift` do? | Promotional Demand Lift: Lift compares demand during a promotion with the normal baseline: 2.0 means sales doubled. |
| 5 | What does `cannibalization_effect` do? | Cannibalization Effect: When a new product steals sales from your own existing one, that steal is cannibalization. |
| 6 | What does `stockout_demand_loss` do? | Stockout Demand Loss: When shelves go empty, some customers wait, some buy a substitute, and some leave forever. |
| 7 | What does `demand_sensing` do? | Demand Sensing: Sensing blends a statistical forecast with the freshest actuals to correct course mid-period. |
| 8 | What does `demand_class_abc_xyz` do? | ABC-XYZ Demand Classification: This combines two lenses: ABC (how much value a product drives) and XYZ (how predictable its demand is). |
| 9 | What does `trend_seasonal_decomposition_forecast` do? | Trend + Seasonal Forecast: This splits history into trend (the slow direction) and season (the repeating wiggle), then puts them back together to forecast forward. |
