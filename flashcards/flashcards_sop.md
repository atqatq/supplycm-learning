# Flashcards: S&OP

3 cards. Cover the answer column, say your answer out loud, then check.
Shuffle the deck once you know the order. Revisit after 1 day, 3 days, 1 week, 1 month.

| # | Prompt | Answer |
|---|--------|--------|
| 1 | What does `demand_supply_match` do? | Demand-Supply Match: This compares your demand plan against your supply plan, period by period, and shows the gaps: where you are short (demand beats supply) and where you build excess (supply beats demand). |
| 2 | What does `production_chase_strategy` do? | Chase Strategy: The chase strategy makes production follow demand exactly: 120 demanded means 120 produced, and inventory stays flat. |
| 3 | What does `production_level_strategy` do? | Level Strategy: The level strategy produces the same amount every period and lets inventory absorb the demand waves. |
