# Recall Drills

A drill is a quiz you take BEFORE you feel ready. It feels harder than reading.
It is also roughly twice as effective - this is the testing effect, one of the
best-replicated findings in learning research.

## How a drill works

1. Answer from memory. No scrolling up. No peeking.
2. Write the answer down - even one honest keyword per question.
3. Check the answer key at the bottom.
4. Score yourself: **1 point per correct answer.**
5. Anything you missed goes back on your flashcard rotation.

## Scoring guide

| Score | Meaning | Action |
|-------|---------|--------|
| 90%+ | Solid | Move on to the next module |
| 70-89% | Shaky edges | Review the misses, re-drill in 3 days |
| Below 70% | Reread the pages | Redo the module's lessons, then re-drill in 1 day |
