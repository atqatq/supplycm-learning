## Part 1 - Questions

**Landed Cost** (`landed_cost`)

1. Why is sticker price a lie in global sourcing?
2. Duty rate rises from 5% to 12% on a cheap import. What lands on your desk?
3. Name two often-forgotten cost lines in imports.

**Total Procurement Cost** (`total_cost_procurement`)

4. Name the six cost buckets in the function.
5. Supplier B is 3% cheaper but doubles defect costs. How do you decide?
6. Which bucket shrinks when you order in bigger, rarer batches?

Total: 6 questions.

## Part 2 - Answer key

1. Freight, duty, insurance, and handling can add 10-30% - the landed number is what hits your books.
2. A new landed cost per unit - often flipping the decision toward the 'expensive' local supplier.
3. Customs broker fees and insurance - small individually, real in total.
4. Purchase, ordering, holding, stockout, quality, admin.
5. Sum total cost for both - the defect line usually eats the 3% and more.
6. Ordering - but holding grows; that tension is exactly what EOQ balances.
