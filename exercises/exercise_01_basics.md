# Exercise 1: Supply Chain Basics

## Problem

Think about a pencil. Trace its supply chain by answering these questions:

1. What raw materials are needed to make a pencil?
2. Where might those materials come from?
3. How does the pencil get from the factory to your hand?
4. What could go wrong at each step?

1. **Materials**: Wood, graphite, rubber (for eraser), metal (for ferrule), paint
2. **Sources**:
   - Wood: forests (cedar trees)
   - Graphite: mines (Sri Lanka, China, Brazil)
   - Rubber: rubber tree plantations (Southeast Asia)
   - Metal: metal suppliers
3. **Journey**: Factory makes pencils -> packs them -> truck to distributor -> distributor to store -> you buy it
4. **What could go wrong**:
   - Bad harvest (wood shortage)
   - Mine closes (graphite shortage)
   - Truck breaks down (delivery delay)
   - Store runs out (you cannot buy)
