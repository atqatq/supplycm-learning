# Quiz 1: Supply Chain Basics

**Questions: 10 | Pass score: 70%**

1. What is a supply chain?
   - a) A type of store
   - b) The journey of a product from raw materials to customer
   - c) A computer network
   - d) A type of truck

2. What are the 5 steps of a supply chain?
   - a) Plan, Source, Make, Deliver, Return
   - b) Buy, Sell, Ship, Return, Refund
   - c) Order, Make, Ship, Sell, Return
   - d) Plan, Buy, Build, Send, Get

3. What is a supplier?
   - a) A customer who buys products
   - b) A company that provides materials or parts
   - c) A type of warehouse
   - d) A delivery truck

4. What is inventory?
   - a) A list of customers
   - b) Stuff kept in storage
   - c) A type of order
   - d) A delivery schedule

5. Why do supply chains matter?
   - a) They do not matter
   - b) They determine if customers can get products
   - c) They are only for big companies
   - d) They are a legal requirement

6. What is the first step in a supply chain?
   - a) Make
   - b) Deliver
   - c) Plan
   - d) Source

7. What is the last step in a supply chain?
   - a) Return
   - b) Plan
   - c) Make
   - d) Source

8. If a truck breaks down, which step is affected?
   - a) Plan
   - b) Source
   - c) Deliver
   - d) Return

9. What does "source" mean in supply chain?
   - a) Finding materials and suppliers
   - b) Selling to customers
   - c) Making the product
   - d) Returning items

10. Give one example of a supply chain from your daily life.
