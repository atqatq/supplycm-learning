# Quiz 2: Forecasting

**Questions: 10 | Pass score: 70%**

1. What is forecasting?
   - a) Predicting the future based on the past
   - b) Making products
   - c) Selling products
   - d) Storing inventory

2. What is the naive forecast method?
   - a) Average the last 3 days
   - b) Tomorrow equals today
   - c) Use complex math
   - d) Ask customers

3. If sales were 40, 50, 45, what is the 3-day moving average?
   - a) 40
   - b) 45
   - c) 50
   - d) 135

4. In exponential smoothing, what does alpha control?
   - a) The number of days to average
   - b) How much weight to give recent data
   - c) The forecast horizon
   - d) The number of seasons

5. What does MAPE stand for?
   - a) Mean Absolute Percentage Error
   - b) Moving Average Prediction Error
   - c) Monthly Average Percentage Estimate
   - d) Multi-Alpha Prediction Engine

6. When would you use Holt-Winters?
   - a) When data has no pattern
   - b) When data has trend and seasonality
   - c) When data is constant
   - d) When you have no historical data

7. What is seasonality?
   - a) A one-time change in demand
   - b) A repeating pattern in data
   - c) A type of forecast error
   - d) A type of inventory

8. If MAPE is 5%, is that good?
   - a) No, it should be 0%
   - b) Yes, less than 10% is great
   - c) No, it should be under 1%
   - d) It depends on the industry

9. What is Croston's method used for?
   - a) Seasonal data
   - b) Intermittent (sparse) demand
   - c) Trending data
   - d) Constant demand

10. Why is forecasting important?
    - a) It is not important
    - b) It helps decide how much to produce or order
    - c) It is required by law
    - d) It replaces inventory management
